# Font log — The Unsaid

Every episode uses its own set of Lipighor (lipighor.com) fonts. Before starting a new episode, check this list and choose fonts that have not been used yet.
Font files stay out of git (licence); they are kept locally in `the-unsaid/assets/fonts/`.

| Episode | Hook text (H) | Supporting lines (B) | Dialogue (Q) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — বংশের বাতি | Li Alinur Sangbadpatra | Li Alinur Tatsama | Li Alinur Nakkhatra |
| 2 — টেবিলক্লথ | Li Alinur Sangbadpatra | Li Alinur Tatsama | Li Alinur Nakkhatra |
| 3 — মাছের লেজ | Li Alinur Kathakali | Li Hasan Jolchobi | Li Alinur Oishee |
| 4 — বাবার গলার আওয়াজ | Li Alinur Shishir | Li Alinur Nilabhro | Li Alinur Subas (+ Li Mahfuz Himadri for the handwritten note) |

Downloaded but not yet used: Li Alinur Snigdha, Li Alinur Tarunya, Li Alinur Priyoshi, Li Alinur Banalata, Li Sabbir Ashalota 1–3, Li Sabbir Sorolota, Li Kaium Simanto, Li Alinur Binoy, Li Alinur Oishee B1/B2.

Visual styles used: episodes 1–3 dark navy cinematic; episode 4 pop poster (bright flat colours). Vary the style too.
