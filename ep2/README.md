# The Unsaid — Episode 2: The Tablecloth (টেবিলক্লথ)

**Video:** `The_Unsaid_EP2_Tablecloth.mp4` (1080×1350, 4:5, 30 fps, about 3:50, H.264 + AAC, −14 LUFS)

Same direction as episode 1 (`../the-unsaid`): the creator's voiceover; kinetic hook text in the Lipighor fonts; flat-vector characters; procedural score and sound effects; "The Unsaid" in the top-left corner and as the end card.

## Visual ideas
- **Colour code:** amber = respectability and the family's image (চাচা, "ভদ্র", "সামাজিকতা"), teal = the sister, rust = hits of truth.
- **The tablecloth:** "সামাজিকতা" cracks in two. A glossy cloth slides over the words নোংরামি, অপরাধ and কান্না, and the dishes are set on top. Stains slowly bleed through.
- **Key moment:** on "...আমার দিকে তাকাইল" the frame and all sound freeze for one second. The cut goes to her point of view: the narrator, framed in an eye shape, holding the jug and smiling.
- **The mouth:** the same tablecloth rises and covers the sister's mouth on "মুখ চাইপা ধরতেছে", while the rest of the family wear smiling masks.
- **Ending:** the smiling mask drops onto the narrator's face and glue drips from it. He tugs at it and it does not come off.
- **Handling of the assault:** shown only as an empty room, a charger cable and the screen closing to darkness. Nothing about it is drawn.
